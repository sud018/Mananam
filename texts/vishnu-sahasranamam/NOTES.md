# Sri Vishnu Sahasranama Stotram – notes

**Text.** The Sanskrit is from the Mahabharata (Anushasana Parva) and is in the public domain.
- It was taken in Devanagari from swami-krishnananda.org (Divine Life Society, Rishikesh), on the pages for invocation, dhyanam, slokas 1–108 and phalasruti.
- It was then converted to Telugu script.
- Small corrections:
  - 57 మహాశృంగః (the page had మహాశంగః)
  - purva 18 శృణు
  - dhyanam 2 వాస్తేయమబ్ధిః
  - dhyanam 7 అంబుదశ్యామమ్
  - phala 25 నమోఽస్తు త
- The speaker headings (యుధిష్ఠిర ఉవాచ, భీష్మ ఉవాచ, అర్జున ఉవాచ …) follow the usual printed editions.

**Check.** All 108 verses were checked against an independent list of the 1000 names, the MIT-licensed data of sahasranama.github.io.
- Every name was found in order.
- The only differences are sandhi, plus spelling slips in that list.
- The meanings give exactly 1000 names over slokas 1–107. Verse 55 counts వినయితా సాక్షీ as one name, as Sri Shankaracharya does.

**Meanings.**
- Written in Telugu for this app, one line per name.
- They follow Sri Shankaracharya's bhashya.
- The purva pithika, dhyanam and phalasruti have a plain Telugu meaning for each verse.

**Not included.** The full anga/kara nyasa lines, which the source page also leaves out, and audio. The app hides the listen and record buttons for this text (`noAudio: true`).
