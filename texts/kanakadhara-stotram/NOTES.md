# Sri Kanakadhara Stotram – notes

**Text.** Adi Shankaracharya's hymn to Lakshmi is in the public domain. It is given in Telugu script, converted from the Devanagari.
- Verses 1–12 and 16–21 come from kamakoti.org.
- Verses 13–15 and the closing phalashruti (సువర్ణధారాస్తోత్రం…) come from the Sanskrit edition on ambuda.org.
- The opening Ganapati verse (వందే వందారుమందారమ్…) and the verse order follow vignanam.org, which is the usual Telugu order.
- Verse 10 reads గీర్దేవతేతి, the common reading; kamakoti.org has ధీర్దేవతేతి.

**Meanings.** Written in simple Telugu for this app, one meaning per verse.

**Structure.** There are four sections: Ganapati prayer; Lakshmi's glance (verses 1–9); salutations (10–15); prayer and phalashruti (16–21 plus the closing verse). There is no audio, and the listen and record buttons are hidden.
